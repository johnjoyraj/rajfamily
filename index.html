📘 Simple User Manual & Guide

Step-by-step guidance on how to use every feature of the Family Portal.

1

How to Log In (पोर्टल में प्रवेश कैसे करें)

Enter your Email and Mobile Number on the login screen. If your mobile number is already recorded in the family directory, you will be verified immediately. Super Admin can manage all records.

2

Viewing the Family Tree (फैमिली ट्री कैसे देखें)

The tree shows ancestral roots at the top. For any person with children, click the yellow "📁 Show Branch" button to expand their children. Click "📂 Hide Branch" anytime to collapse and keep the screen clean.

3

Adding a New Family Member or Baby (नया सदस्य या बच्चा कैसे जोड़ें)

Click the green "+ Add Family Member" button at the top of the Family Tree or Directory. A popup window will appear right on your screen. Select the Parent / Upper Level Link from the dropdown, enter the Full Name, Mobile Number, Birth Date, and Sequence Order (1 for 1st child, 2 for 2nd child), then click "Save Family Member".

4

Updating or Editing Details (जानकारी कैसे सुधारें)

Find your name or your child's name in the Family Tree or Directory. Click the orange "Edit" button on that card. The popup window will open with all details filled in. Update the information (new mobile number, wedding date, city, bio) and click "Save Family Member".

5

Direct WhatsApp Chat (व्हाट्सएप पर बात करें)

Every card has a green "💬 WhatsApp" button for both the member and their spouse. Tapping it opens a direct WhatsApp chat window with a friendly connection greeting. You can also join the main Family WhatsApp Group via the Live Message Board.

6

Using the Live Community Message Board (मैसेज बोर्ड का उपयोग)

Switch to "📢 Live Message Board" to share announcements, wedding news, birthday wishes, or prayer requests. Messages appear immediately for all family members.

7

Birthdays & Anniversaries (जन्मदिन और सालगिरह)

Check the right sidebar to see all upcoming birthdays and wedding anniversaries happening this month. Never miss a family celebration!
